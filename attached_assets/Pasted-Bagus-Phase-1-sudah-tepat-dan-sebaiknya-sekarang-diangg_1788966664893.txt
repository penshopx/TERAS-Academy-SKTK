# OBJECTIVE

Build:

> **Personal Diagnostic & Readiness Map**

The purpose is to transform TERAS SKTTK from a generic learning environment into a personalized competency preparation system.

The learner must be able to answer:

1. What SKTTK target am I preparing for?
2. What do I already know?
3. What can I already do?
4. What is my experience level?
5. What are my safety strengths and gaps?
6. What evidence do I already have?
7. What competency areas need development?
8. What should I learn next?
9. What should I practice next?
10. What is currently preventing me from being ready?

# IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Do NOT create a single "pre-test score".

Diagnostic is NOT merely an exam.

It is a:

> **Personal Competency & Readiness Diagnostic**

The output must be a structured learner profile.

---

# DIAGNOSTIC DIMENSIONS

Create these dimensions:

## 1. KNOWLEDGE

Measure:

* technical concepts;
* terminology;
* principles;
* procedures;
* calculations where applicable;
* interpretation;
* troubleshooting knowledge.

---

## 2. PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE

Measure:

* relevant work experience;
* frequency of performing tasks;
* level of independence;
* exposure to actual projects;
* familiarity with tools/equipment;
* experience solving technical problems.

---

## 3. PRACTICAL CAPABILITY

Measure the learner's ability to:

* identify equipment;
* sequence procedures;
* perform technical tasks;
* troubleshoot;
* make technical decisions;
* follow procedures;
* explain technical work.

---

## 4. SAFETY

Safety must be a separate dimension.

Measure:

* hazard identification;
* electrical safety awareness;
* PPE;
* isolation procedures;
* safe working sequence;
* unsafe condition recognition;
* emergency response;
* safety decision-making.

Do NOT merge Safety into a general average score.

---

## 5. COMMUNICATION

Measure:

* technical explanation;
* procedure explanation;
* reasoning;
* justification of technical decisions;
* ability to communicate safety considerations.

---

## 6. EXISTING EVIDENCE

Ask what the learner already has:

* work experience records;
* training records;
* practice records;
* project documentation;
* technical reports;
* photographs/videos where applicable;
* previous competency-related records.

Important:

These are learner-provided preparation references.

They are NOT automatically official certification evidence.

### Kenapa saya menyarankan ini sebagai Phase 2

Karena setelah Phase 1, **TERAS SKTTK sudah memiliki identitas dan boundary yang benar, tetapi belum memiliki "otak personalisasi"**.

Diagnostic akan menjadi penghubung:

**Learner → Competency → LMS**

Sehingga nanti LMS tidak lagi sekadar:

> “Ini daftar materi yang harus Anda pelajari.”

tetapi:

> **“Berdasarkan kondisi Anda, inilah kompetensi yang perlu diperkuat, materi yang perlu dipelajari, praktik yang harus dilakukan, evidence yang perlu dibangun, dan simulasi yang perlu dilewati.”**

Kemudian **Phase 3** baru kita masukkan **SKTTK Competency Framework** secara serius. Di situlah kita mulai membangun hubungan:

> **Unit Kompetensi → Elemen → KUK → Knowledge → Practice → Evidence → Safety → Simulation**

Setelah itu Phase 4 menjadi **Readiness Rules Engine**, sehingga seluruh hasil tersebut akhirnya benar-benar menggerakkan **Readiness Cockpit**.

Dengan urutan ini, kita menjaga prinsip TERAS yang sudah Anda bangun: **jangan merusak mesin yang sudah lulus 91/91 test, tetapi tambahkan lapisan domain SKTTK secara bertahap dan terukur.**
